Privacy

The trainer sends nothing anywhere: your settings and progress are stored only in the browser on your device.

This site counts visits with Yandex Metrica, a service of Yandex. The counter only starts if you press “Accept”.

What Metrica sees: which pages are opened, where you came from, your device and browser, your approximate city from the IP address, clicks and scrolling. Session replay records actions on the page, but there is nothing to type here — the site has no forms. Yandex stores the data under its Metrica terms of use.

How to opt out: press “Visit counter” at the bottom of the page and choose “No”. You can also block cookies in your browser settings.
