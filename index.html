Read music fluently

A sight-reading trainer for digital pianos. Connect it by cable to a tablet, phone or computer, and play.

Open the trainer Free. No sign-up.

Read in phrases, not note by note

Sequences
Find the first note from a landmark note, then move by intervals. Hints fade as you get it right.
Contour
Play the shape of the melody — up, down, same — from any key.
Rhythm
Tap out the rhythm on any key.

Tricky spots come up more often, and after each session you see what got better.

Large. At your own pace.

  • Big notes and honest contrast, for anyone who struggles with small print.
  • No countdown, no “fail”: your speed is measured quietly.
  • No piano nearby? Play on the on-screen keyboard.

Connect your piano and play

This browser cannot work with the piano. Open the site in Chrome.

  1. Connect the piano to your device with a USB cable.
  2. Open the trainer in Chrome.
  3. Allow access to the piano.
  4. Press “Start”.

What you need

A digital piano with USB. An Android tablet or phone (with an OTG adapter if the port doesn’t fit) or a Windows, macOS or Linux computer. Chrome or Edge.

iPhone and iPad won’t work: Safari has no access to the piano. You can try the on-screen keyboard on any device.

Honestly

  • After the first visit the trainer works offline.
  • The trainer collects nothing: your progress stays on your device.
  • This site counts visits — see “Privacy”.
